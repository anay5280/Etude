## Description
```
Etude teaches you to play the piano one note at a time — then listens as you play and tells you exactly what to fix.

Pick a piece and move through three phases:
• Demonstrate — hear the piece so you know how it should sound.
• Learn — note by note, Etude shows the next key, waits for you to play it, and only moves on when you get it right. Miss it, and it tells you precisely how far off you were and which way to move.
• Practice — play the whole piece for a score.

PLAY HOWEVER YOU LIKE
• Tap the on-screen keys, or
• Turn on the mic and play a real acoustic or digital piano — Etude follows along, ignores background talking, and keeps up with fast playing.

MELODIES AND CHORDS
Practice single-note melodies or full chord progressions, with clear feedback on missing and extra notes.

BRING YOUR OWN SONGS
Import MIDI or MusicXML files and learn them the same way.

RECORD & TRANSCRIBE
Play something and let Etude transcribe it into a practiceable piece — using an on-device neural model. No internet required.

KEEP A DAILY STREAK
Finish a lesson each day to build a practice streak — a little nudge to keep at it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, and your audio is never recorded to disk or uploaded.
```
